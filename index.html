Features

One job. Done well.

Compress, resize and optimize comic archives for your e-reader. Pick your device, pick your output format, hit Optimize.

  • Smart compression Dramatically smaller files while keeping the visual quality.
  • Device presets Kindle Paperwhite, Kindle Scribe, Kobo Libra Colour, Kobo Sage, reMarkable Paper Pro, and more, or set a custom resolution. ComicsFit resizes every page to fit the screen exactly.
  • Manga mode Right-to-left page order for KEPUB and AZW3 output, so your manga reads correctly on the device.
  • Trim borders Removes the empty margins around scanned pages so the artwork uses the whole screen.
  • Black & white Converts pages to grayscale for black and white screens: even smaller files, no visible difference.
  • Batch processing Queue up your whole collection and walk away.
  • Truly native Built with Swift on macOS and GTK4/Adwaita on Linux. Fast and light at ~20 MB.

FAQ

Questions

Is ComicsFit free?

Yes. Every format, device preset and option is free, one file at a time. A one-time €9 license unlocks the batch queue so you can convert a whole collection in one go.

Which format should I use for my e-reader?

KEPUB for Kobo, AZW3 for Kindle and PDF for reMarkable. Pick your device in ComicsFit and it only shows the formats that work on it, with the best one selected.

Can I read manga right-to-left?

Yes. Turn on manga mode and ComicsFit sets right-to-left page order in KEPUB (Kobo) and AZW3 (Kindle) files, so swiping goes the way the manga was drawn.

How much smaller do the files get?

It depends on the source, but scans are often much bigger than an e-reader screen. Resizing to the exact screen resolution and recompressing commonly saves 50 to 90%, with no visible difference on the device.

Are my comics uploaded anywhere?

No. ComicsFit is a desktop app and every conversion runs on your computer. No account, no upload.

Does ComicsFit work on Windows?

Not yet. ComicsFit is available for macOS (Apple Silicon) and Linux. A Windows version is planned.
