Comics and manga on the Kindle Scribe 3

The Kindle Scribe 3 has a 1986 × 2648 pixel black and white screen. ComicsFit resizes every page of your CBZ, CBR or EPUB comics to exactly that size, compresses them and converts them to AZW3, the format that works best on Kindle.

How to

Optimize comics for the Kindle Scribe 3

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr, .epub or .kepub files into the window.
  3. Select Kindle Scribe 3 in the device list. The resolution is set for you.
  4. Choose AZW3 as output format. For manga, turn on Manga mode. Turn on Black & White for smaller files: the screen is grayscale anyway.
  5. Click Convert.
Why AZW3

The right format for Kindle

AZW3 (KF8) is the Kindle's own format for fixed-layout books. Pages open full screen, page turns are fast, and manga mode sets right-to-left reading so volumes read the way they were drawn.

Resizing matters too: comic scans are often two or three times larger than the screen. Shrinking each page to 1986 × 2648 makes files much smaller and page turns faster, with no visible loss on the e-reader.

Transfer

Put the comic on your Kindle Scribe 3

  1. Connect your Kindle to your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Copy the .azw3 file into the documents folder on the Kindle.
  3. Eject the Kindle. The comic appears in your library.

Send to Kindle doesn't accept AZW3 files, so use a USB cable. On macOS, some recent Kindles don't show up as a drive. A free tool like OpenMTP lets you copy files to them.
