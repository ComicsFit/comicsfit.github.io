Comics and manga on the Kobo Libra Colour

The Kobo Libra Colour has a 1264 × 1680 pixel color screen. ComicsFit resizes every page of your CBZ, CBR or EPUB comics to exactly that size, compresses them and converts them to KEPUB, the format that works best on Kobo.

How to

Optimize comics for the Kobo Libra Colour

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr, .epub or .kepub files into the window.
  3. Select Kobo Libra Colour in the device list. The resolution is set for you.
  4. Choose KEPUB as output format. For manga, turn on Manga mode.
  5. Click Convert.
Why KEPUB

The right format for Kobo

KEPUB opens in the Kobo's native reading engine: page turns are faster than with plain EPUB or CBZ, and manga mode sets right-to-left reading.

The Kobo Libra Colour shows comics in color, so keep Black & White off to preserve the colors.

Resizing matters too: comic scans are often two or three times larger than the screen. Shrinking each page to 1264 × 1680 makes files much smaller and page turns faster, with no visible loss on the e-reader.

Transfer

Put the comic on your Kobo Libra Colour

  1. Connect your Kobo to your computer with a USB cable and tap Connect on the e-reader.
  2. Copy the .kepub.epub file anywhere on the KOBOeReader drive.
  3. Eject the drive. The comic appears in My Books.

Keep the .kepub.epub extension that ComicsFit adds: it tells the Kobo to use its KEPUB engine. Kobo also supports EPUB and CBZ if you prefer.
