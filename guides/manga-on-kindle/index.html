Read manga on Kindle, right to left

Manga is read right to left, but most converted volumes open left to right on a Kindle. ComicsFit converts your volumes to AZW3 with Manga mode on, so swiping goes the way the manga was drawn.

↓ Download for macOS
How to

Manga to Kindle in five steps

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Windows or Linux and open it.
  2. Drop your manga volumes (.cbz, .cbr or .epub) into the window.
  3. Select your Kindle in the device list.
  4. Keep AZW3 and turn on Manga mode. On a black and white Kindle, also turn on Black & White: most manga is grayscale anyway, and files get smaller.
  5. Click Convert. With a license, queue a whole series at once.
Tips

Getting manga to look right

  • Trim borders Scans often have wide white margins. Trimming them makes the art larger on a 6 or 7 inch screen.
  • Double-page spreads Wide spreads are kept whole and sized to fill the screen when you turn the Kindle sideways.
  • Smaller volumes Pages are resized to your Kindle's exact resolution, so a 200 MB volume often ends up a fraction of that, and page turns are faster.

Transfer

Put it on your Kindle

  1. Connect your Kindle to your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Copy the .azw3 file into the documents folder on the Kindle.
  3. Eject the Kindle. The comic appears in your library.

Send to Kindle doesn't accept AZW3 files, so use a USB cable. On macOS, some recent Kindles don't show up as a drive. A free tool like OpenMTP lets you copy files to them.
