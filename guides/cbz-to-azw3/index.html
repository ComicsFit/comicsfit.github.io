Convert CBZ to AZW3 for Kindle

Kindles can't open CBZ or CBR comics. They need AZW3, Amazon's format for fixed-layout books. ComicsFit converts your comics to AZW3 in one click, sized for your Kindle's screen, without Kindle Previewer or KindleGen.

How to

CBZ to AZW3 in five steps

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Windows or Linux and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr or .epub files into the window.
  3. Select your Kindle in the device list. The resolution is set for you.
  4. Keep AZW3 as output format. Turn on Manga mode for right-to-left books, and Black & White on a grayscale Kindle for smaller files.
  5. Click Convert.
No KindleGen

Why you don't need Kindle Previewer

Most comic converters, like Kindle Comic Converter, hand the final step to Amazon's KindleGen. Amazon no longer offers KindleGen on its own: it only ships inside Kindle Previewer, a download of about 1 GB. ComicsFit has its own AZW3 (KF8) writer, so the whole conversion happens in one 4 MB app.

Why AZW3

AZW3 vs MOBI and PDF on Kindle

AZW3 opens each page full screen with fast page turns, and supports right-to-left reading for manga. MOBI is the older format it replaced. PDF works on Kindle too, but pages are rendered small with margins, except on the large Kindle Scribe, where ComicsFit also offers PDF.

Transfer

Put it on your Kindle

  1. Connect your Kindle to your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Copy the .azw3 file into the documents folder on the Kindle.
  3. Eject the Kindle. The comic appears in your library.

Send to Kindle doesn't accept AZW3 files, so use a USB cable. On macOS, some recent Kindles don't show up as a drive. A free tool like OpenMTP lets you copy files to them.
