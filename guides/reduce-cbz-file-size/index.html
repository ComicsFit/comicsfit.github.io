Reduce CBZ file size

Comic scans are often two or three times larger than an e-reader screen. ComicsFit resizes every page to your device's exact resolution and recompresses it: files commonly shrink by 50 to 90%, with no visible difference on the device.

↓ Download for macOS
How to

Compress comics in five steps

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Windows or Linux and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr or .epub files into the window.
  3. Select your e-reader, or set a custom resolution for a tablet or phone.
  4. Pick an output format. Keep CBZ (choose a Kobo or Custom device) if you just want a smaller CBZ.
  5. Click Convert. ComicsFit shows how much space you saved.
Where the savings come from

Three ways to smaller comics

  • Resize to the screen The biggest win. A 3000 pixel tall scan shown on a 1680 pixel screen wastes most of its pixels. ComicsFit never upscales: smaller pages stay as they are.
  • Black & White On a grayscale e-reader, color data is wasted. Converting to grayscale cuts the size further.
  • Trim borders Removes empty margins, so the art takes the whole screen instead of white space.
