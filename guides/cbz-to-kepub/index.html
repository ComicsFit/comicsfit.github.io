Convert CBZ and CBR to KEPUB for Kobo

Kobo e-readers open CBZ and EPUB, but page turns are slow. KEPUB uses Kobo's own reading engine: pages turn faster and manga can read right to left. ComicsFit converts your comics to KEPUB, sized for your Kobo.

↓ Download for macOS
How to

CBZ to KEPUB in five steps

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Windows or Linux and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr, .epub or .kepub files into the window.
  3. Select your Kobo in the device list. The resolution is set for you.
  4. Keep KEPUB as output format. On a black and white Kobo, turn on Black & White for smaller files. On a Kobo Libra Colour or Clara Colour, leave it off.
  5. Click Convert.
Why KEPUB

KEPUB vs CBZ and EPUB on Kobo

CBZ and plain EPUB open in Kobo's older reader, which is noticeably slower with large images. KEPUB opens in the same engine as books from the Kobo store: faster page turns, reading stats, and right-to-left page order for manga. The file keeps the .kepub.epub extension so the Kobo recognizes it.

Transfer

Put it on your Kobo

  1. Connect your Kobo to your computer with a USB cable and tap Connect on the e-reader.
  2. Copy the .kepub.epub file anywhere on the KOBOeReader drive.
  3. Eject the drive. The comic appears in My Books.

Keep the .kepub.epub extension that ComicsFit adds: it tells the Kobo to use its KEPUB engine. Kobo also supports EPUB and CBZ if you prefer.
