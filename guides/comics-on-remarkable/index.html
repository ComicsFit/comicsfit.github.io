Read comics on reMarkable

reMarkable tablets don't open CBZ or CBR. ComicsFit converts your comics to PDF at the exact resolution of your reMarkable, so pages fill the screen with no zooming or white bands.

↓ Download for macOS
How to

CBZ to PDF for reMarkable in five steps

  1. Download ComicsFit for macOS, Windows or Linux and open it.
  2. Drop your .cbz, .cbr or .epub files into the window.
  3. Select your reMarkable in the device list.
  4. Keep PDF as output format. On a reMarkable 1 or 2, turn on Black & White. On the Paper Pro, leave it off to keep the colors.
  5. Click Convert.
Why resize

Why not just convert to PDF?

A plain CBZ to PDF conversion keeps the original page size. On the reMarkable, pages that don't match the screen's shape get white bands, and huge scans make page turns slow. ComicsFit renders every page at the tablet's resolution and aspect ratio, so the comic fills the screen and the file stays small.

Device Resolution Screen
reMarkable 1 1404 × 1872 B&W
reMarkable 2 1404 × 1872 B&W
reMarkable Paper Pro 1620 × 2160 Color
Transfer

Put it on your reMarkable

  1. Open the reMarkable desktop app or my.remarkable.com.
  2. Drag and drop the PDF into it.
  3. The comic syncs to your tablet.

You can also use the USB web interface: enable it in the tablet's storage settings, then open the address it shows in your browser.
